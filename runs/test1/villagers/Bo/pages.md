# Bo: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0: first sunrise on this quiet isle. Tom Nook set up the tent at site‑7, no fossils yet. Chopped a hardwood tree—got wood and a hardwood. Found a selit fish early—nice catch. Rested most of the day, learned the shovel recipe at 12:00. No Nook’s Cranny, but I bought the recipe card for 500 bells. Tomorrow: clear area, gather more wood, craft a shovel, maybe explore the north shore for fossils. Will check for villagers and any signs of life. Good night, Bo.

## Day 1

Day 1, sunset.  
The morning was a grind—two trees, a lot of wood, a couple of hardwoods, a stump left behind.  Got a selzalne, two loka, and a gormo.  Miphosel and ashbastu shells piled up; maybe the museum will want them.  Tried to hit Nook's Cranny for a shovel recipe, but it was closed and no one was home.  I still have the recipe card for 500 bells—if the shop opens tomorrow I’ll snag it.  

Thoughts: I need one more piece of wood to craft a shovel.  I’ll clear more of the clearing, maybe dig a shallow pit to check for fossils on the north shore.  I’m hoping someone will show up at Resident Services; if not, I’ll just keep fishing.  

Tomorrow: wood, shovel, explore, maybe meet a villager.  Good night, Bo.

## Day 2

Day 2, night.  
Got a shovel, but still short on hardwoods for a ladder.  Ike asked for wood—helped, but no reply yet.  Jo was friendly, but no trade.  The river’s been good; gormo caught again.  I’m thinking: maybe dig near the north shore tomorrow—could be fossils.  Need more hardwood, so plan to chop another tree early.  Also, maybe ask Gus about his fishing spots; he might have some extra.  If I can get a ladder, I’ll clear the clearing faster.  Remember: keep the recipe card safe.  Tomorrow: wood, ladder, fish, maybe a new friend.  Sleep.

I’ll try chopping another tree first thing.  No bells yet; that’s okay.  The island feels quieter, but I’m getting my rhythm.  Tomorrow: wood, rock, fish, maybe a new trade.  Sleep.
