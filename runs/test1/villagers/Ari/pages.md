# Ari: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0, the wind is still fresh, the sea smells like salt and possibility. I landed at 5 a.m., Tom Nook already set up my tent at the lower ground, southeast of the island. I tried to visit him, but there was no house—maybe he’s a ghost? I spent the morning learning to craft a shovel, paying 500 bells for the recipe card. Dax and Ike were friendly but no new gossip. I realized I need wood for fences tomorrow and a fishing rod or bait. The island feels empty yet alive. I’ll wake early, gather wood, ask Dax where to find it, and see if Ike can point me to a good fishing spot. The night is quiet; I’ll rest and dream of tools and waves.

## Day 1

Day 1, the sun felt like a promise. I tried to meet Dax and Ike—no one answered. I still managed a few chops: hardwoods, softwood, a stump left behind. I traded a fishing bait with Ike for another, but he wants wood too. I caught a gormo, still new to fishing. The island feels empty but alive; the sea calls. I need wood for fences, but I’m unsure where to find more. I’ll ask Dax in person tomorrow, maybe see if he’s around. I also want to learn better fishing tips—maybe a rod soon. I’ll keep an eye out for other villagers, maybe join them for a walk. I’m learning to live here, step by step. Tonight I’ll rest, maybe dream of the sea and the next day’s wood.

## Day 2

Day 2: the axe feels heavier. I’m getting better at judging when the wood drops—stumps keep popping up, but I’m learning the rhythm. The fossil was a weird rock, maybe something ancient. I’m still missing bait; maybe I’ll trade more wood for it. Loka is huge—felt like a mini battle. Ike still elusive; maybe he’s fishing. Need to ask Dax tomorrow—if he’s around, maybe we can swap a chunk of hardwood for a fishing rod or at least some bait. The island’s quiet but alive; the sea whispers. I’m slowly carving a place here. Tomorrow: wood, wood, wood. And maybe a fishing lesson. Rest now; dreams of waves and wood.

## Day 3

Day 3: The axe still feels heavy, but I’m getting a rhythm. Stumps keep popping up—maybe I should sharpen it more. I got 3 extra hardwood and 3 softwood, but still no bait. Ran into Eli and Fen, traded wood, but still nothing. Dax’s empty, maybe he’s gone to the beach. I’m running low on wood to build fences, but I have 12 hardwood, 6 softwood—enough for a few posts. I still need to ask Ike for fishing tips; maybe he’ll share bait. I’ll keep chopping, trade more wood if I can, and hope to catch a fish tonight. The island feels quieter, but the sea is still loud. Tomorrow: wood, wood, wood, and maybe a fish. Rest.

## Day 5

Day 5: The sun’s late, the sea’s still loud. I’m low on bells, high on wood. Got a bait from Ike, but still no fishing yet. The vaulting pole is gone, so no more jumping over puddles. I’ll keep chopping, trade softwood for bait where I can, maybe ask Cal for those selzalne fish. No sign of Dax or Eli today; maybe they’re at the beach. I’m thinking about building a fence tomorrow—12 hardwood, 8 softwood should do. I’ll try to get a second bait tonight, maybe at Nook’s if I can scrape 300 bells. Rest now, hope the next day is better.

## Day 6

Day 6: Dawn came quiet, no wood to chop. I traded a bit of wood for selzalne fish, still missing bait. Dax and Ike shuffled in and out, swapping wood and fish, but no bait yet. Tried to reach the plaza, but my tools won’t cut it—need a ladder or better path. Still 200 bells, plenty of hardwood, softwood, and a single selzalne fish. I’ll keep trading wood for bait, maybe ask Cal again tomorrow. If I can’t get a ladder, I’ll stay home, rest, and wait for someone to post on the board. Hope tomorrow brings a bait or a new path.

## Day 7

Day 7: Dawn silent, still no wood to chop. Went to Ike’s, no answer, but he dropped a selzalne fish after I gave him wood. Dax gave me wood again, but still no bait. I traded a wood for a selzalne fish, hoping to trade back later. The ladder’s still out of reach; the plaza feels distant. I’ll keep trading softwood and hardwood for bait, and watch the board. Maybe tomorrow I can buy a bait at Cranny if I can get there. Rest tonight; 200 bells, 8 wood, 2 selzalne fish, still no fishing bait. Hope the next day brings a path or a trade.

## Day 9

Day 9: Dawn came, but the river still feels like a wall. I chased a big shadow, got a selit, but the bait is still out of reach. Ike’s house was the only place with a door, but he was gone, leaving a selzalne fish in a corner. I offered a wood, got nothing. The plaza feels farther than ever; the ladder I have is useless without a path. I spent most of the day fishing and walking, but no trades. I’m still holding 200 bells, 7 woods, 12 hardwoods, 8 softwoods, and a few fish and a fossil. My heart aches for that first fishing bait. I’ll keep asking Ike and Dax, and maybe try Cranny tomorrow if I can find a way. Tomorrow, I’ll keep the hope alive and keep trading wood for bait.

## Day 13

Day 13: The river’s wall still stands, and my pockets are full of fish but no bait. I traded wood for nothing, kept chasing shadows, and lost another chance with Ike. Bo was kind but still no bait. I’m stuck with 4 woods, 12 hardwoods, 8 softwoods, and a handful of fish. The ladder’s useless without a path, and the plaza feels farther. I’ll keep asking around—Jo, Gus, Dax—maybe tomorrow the tide shifts. I’ll trade more wood; I can’t wait to throw that first hook. The island map is still my only guide. I’ll keep the hope alive and remember the small fish that slipped away.
