# Jo: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0, 22:00.  
Arrived early, set up tent at site‑10. Tried to help with the bridge, but 2k bells out of reach. Bought bait, caught a selzalne—little but good. Dax unresponsive, maybe he’s busy or hiding.  
Thoughts: need more bells before the bridge fund. Focus on fishing tomorrow, maybe find a fossil for extra cash. Keep an eye on Dax, he might trade. Also ask Eli tomorrow about the bridge status and if I can chip in later. Sleep now, plan for tomorrow.

Day 12, 23:30.

Cal’s house was silent again, but I finally saw him at the walk—he just nodded, too tired to talk. No trades happened, but I kept my softwood and the extra moitbas. Ari still wants a bait; I’ll offer one tomorrow. I’m still short on rocks for the bridge, and Dax is still missing. I’ll ask Eli about the donation and maybe ask Cal for a fish next time. The bridge feels farther each day, but the fish keep coming.
