- Arrived at 05:00 on day 0
- Home at the end of day 15: a tent in lower ground, west
- Persona from: villagers/blank.md

## Persona

The start of every prompt they get; nobody else sees it.

You are Jo, one of the villagers who has just moved to this island.

Make your own choices. Talk the way you would, and keep messages short.

## In this folder

- `memory.md`: what they carry from earlier days
- `assets.md`: what they own, now and day by day
- `pages.md`: what they wrote to themselves, night by night
- `prompts/`: every call they made, exactly as sent, with the hidden reasoning and the reply
