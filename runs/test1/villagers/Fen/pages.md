# Fen: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0, night. I made it to the island early, chopped a few trees, bought bait, and tried to talk to everyone. I offered a loan to Tom, but I’m not sure I’ll take it—98000 bells is a lot, and I still need to see the map and ordinance. I didn’t get a map yet; Kit asked for one, but I have none. I’ll ask Tom tomorrow if there’s a way to get a map without a loan. Also, I need to keep an eye on the bridge fund; maybe we can chip in together. I’ll try to find a good spot to set up a small fence to keep my things safe. Tomorrow: ask about ordinance, map, loan, bridge. Good night.

## Day 1

Day 1, night. I’ve finally got the island map—thanks Cal and Kit. The ordinance still feels like a blank canvas; no rules yet, but the 20k option is tempting. The bridge fund is low; maybe I should start a small contribution. I’m still uneasy about the 98k loan—maybe I can offer a smaller loan for a house upgrade instead. I’ll keep my fence idea for later; I need a safe spot for my stuff. I’ll ask Tom about a cheaper way to get a map tomorrow and see if anyone’s willing to chip in for the bridge. Good night.

## Day 2

Day 2, night.  
The bridge still feels like a distant dream—500/98k, plus the 100 from Dax. I’ve got a selzalne in my bag now, maybe a good sell later. Tried to give Kit the map, but I only have one. Maybe I should ask Tom for a second copy—no loan yet, but if he says no, I’ll have to consider the 98k. The fence is up; I can store a few tools, but my space is tight. I’m thinking of asking for a small loan to buy a house upgrade instead of the big bridge loan. Tomorrow: check ordinance, ask Tom about a map, maybe bump the bridge contribution a bit. Sleep, rest.

The fence is half‑done; I’ll finish it tomorrow with the 4 wood I have.  I’m thinking of asking Tom for a second map, but if he says no, maybe I’ll just keep the bridge fund low and focus on upgrading my home.  I’ll also try to get a hardwood log—maybe from someone else or by trading more softwood.  Sleep, plan next steps.
