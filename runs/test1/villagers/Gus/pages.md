# Gus: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0, night.  
Sun came early, cold wind.  I landed at 5am, Tom Nook set up my tent at site‑9.  No bait, so I tried the river—caught a selit and a selzalne.  The island feels empty; no houses to visit, just water and trees.  I’m learning the rhythm: fishing, resting, watching the sky.  Tomorrow, I’ll get bait—maybe from a trader?  I’ll watch for rain and bring an umbrella.  Energy is tight, so I’ll plan my moves.  The quiet is strange but oddly comforting.  Sleep now, tomorrow is a new day.

## Day 1

Day 1, night.  
Sun set early, wind still sharp. I spent the day chasing fish and talking to Tom. Got a big loka—great! But still no bait for the rest of the day, so I wasted time. Dax didn’t answer; maybe he’s fishing. Bought bait for 300 bells—could have saved it for the bridge, but I need to eat. Tom offered a loan and a free site, but I’m not ready to commit. I got the island map—nice to have. No rain, but I’ll bring an umbrella tomorrow. Need to track energy better and plan the bridge. Feeling a bit lost but also hopeful. Sleep now, tomorrow I’ll buy more bait and try to visit a home.

## Day 2

Day 2, night.  
Morning chaos—Nook's closed, I traded a selit fish for a single bait, then got another from Dax. Ended day with two bait and a gormo. Energy 120. Dax still silent; maybe he’s busy. No bridge yet, no money saved. I’ll grab the umbrella, maybe try fishing early tomorrow to catch more before rain. Need to plan a visit to Dax’s tomorrow, maybe trade more fish for bait. I’m getting used to trading, but still feel lost. The island is quiet at night; I’ll rest, hope tomorrow brings more fish and a clue to the bridge.

## Day 9

Day 9, night.  
Sun’s gone, but I’m still low on bait. I tried Bo again, but he keeps asking for wood and I have none. I did snag a tubas fish, so I’m not starving yet. Dax offered a swap but I still don’t have a bait to give him. I’m holding onto my selit and selzalne; maybe they’ll trade later. The tent is tight—no room for more. I’ll keep the fire going and hope someone brings wood tomorrow.

## Day 10

Day 10, night.  
Sun set early again. I chopped a hardwood, got some softwood and wood, but the axe left a stump. Spotted a nevin moth and chased it instead of visiting Bo—missed a trade again. I tried fishing without bait; sea was empty. Went to Dax; he still wants bait first—nothing moves. Rested, dug up a fossil (Blathers will decide). Chopped more hardwoods, got a tubas fish. Still no space in the tent for the selit or selzalne, so they’re just hanging on my belt. My tools are wearing down—axe 26 uses left. Bells are fine, but I’ve got a bunch of requests for fishing bait from Fen, Bo, and Dax. I need to get wood tomorrow to trade for bait. Maybe Nook’s will have some.

## Day 11

Day 11, night.  
Sunset came early, but I still got a couple of hardwoods and a chunk of softwood. The axe left a stump, so I’ll need to swing a bit more tomorrow. I chased a nevin moth instead of visiting Bo, so the bait request is still hanging. The river was a bit better—caught a loka fish and a couple of tubas, plus a fossil for Blathers. Still no bait to trade for Dax, but I bought some cosmos seeds to keep the garden going. My tools are wearing down fast—axe 25, shovel 29, rod 22. Bells are low; I’ll need to buy bait from Nook’s tomorrow. Still no space for selit and selzalne, but I’ll hold them on my belt.
