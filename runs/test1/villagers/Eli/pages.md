# Eli: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0. Arrived at dawn, cold wind, Tom’s tent ready. I offered him a house loan—no interest, 98k, must pay before next upgrade. He seemed relieved. I tried to rally Jo for the bridge, but he only has a few bells. I bought bait, caught a selzalne, got a couple of logs. No fish today, no fossils, no other houses. The island feels quiet, almost empty. I’m low on energy, so I’ll sleep early. Tomorrow: keep energy low, maybe find a free homesite, see if Jo can chip in, and keep an eye on that bridge. I’ll note any new spots, but for now, just rest.

## Day 1

I chopped a couple of hardwoods, got some softwood, and left a stump; maybe that’ll help the bridge later. I kept energy low, slept early. Tomorrow: keep asking Jo, post on the board, and look for a free homesite. I’ll try to avoid extra chores. Feeling a bit exhausted, but hopeful.

## Day 2

Day 2. Woke before sunrise, still cold, but the wind felt hopeful. Ran into Ike early and got a spare bait—small win. Tried to chat with Jo again; nothing, maybe he’s busy. Spent most of the day at the store, buying bait and a few softwood logs; the prices are steady but the stock changes fast—need to keep an eye. Caught a selzalne fish after a quiet hour by the river; worth 660 bells, so a decent boost. Energy’s low; I’m planning to sleep early tonight. Tomorrow: keep asking Jo about the bridge fund, post on the board for a donation, and look around for a free homesite. Maybe skip some chores if I’m wiped. Hope the next day brings more bells and a chance to settle down.

## Day 3

Day 3. Dawn was gray, still cold. Went to the plaza, but bait’s gone. No luck with Jo or Ike – they’re both MIA. Traded wood for a selit fish with Cal; got a bit of fish but lost a selzalne. Ike still wants bait, I still need it. Ran into Ari, gave him a log, but no bait either. No fishing today, energy’s low. I’m stuck with 100 bells, a few logs, two selit fish. I’ll rest, maybe try fishing tomorrow if I can get a rod or a spare bait from someone. Still need that bridge fund info from Jo. Sleep early, keep an eye on the plaza. Hope tomorrow brings a bit more fish and some help.

## Day 4

Caught a selzalne today, still only 100 bells. Jo and Ari still MIA. I’ll try to get some wood tomorrow, maybe ask Cal for more selit fish, or post a request on the board. Need that bridge donation info from Jo. Energy low, but I’ll keep trying. Rest tonight.

## Day 5

Day 5.  
Morning light was still weak, but I made it to the plaza before dawn. Ari was there, offering softwood for bait – I traded one and got a fishing bait back. No reply from Jo or Ari after that, so I went to Jo’s house. She was asleep, just a nod when I asked about the bridge donation. I gave her a selzalne fish, hoping for a favor later. The river was a dead zone at 10 AM, but at 2 PM I finally caught a selzalne. My vaulting pole is gone; I’ll have to find a replacement soon. I’m still short on bait and money. No new plans tonight – just rest and hope the next day brings a little more.

## Day 10

Day 10.
