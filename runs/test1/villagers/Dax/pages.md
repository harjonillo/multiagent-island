# Dax: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0, the island feels like a blank slate. Tom Nook set up my tent at site‑13, and I offered him a house loan—maybe I’ll build it myself if I can afford the 98k. No Resident Services yet, so I’m still in the dark about ordinances, but Cal’s curiosity keeps me on the lookout. I’m gathering wood, fossils, and a fishing bait—could be useful. I’m not sure where the museum is, so I’ll ask Blathers tomorrow. The bridge to the north bank is expensive, but it could open up a lot of resources. I need to keep my eyes peeled for any council announcements and find a map. Sleep now; tomorrow is another day of exploration.

## Day 1

Day 1: Still feeling like a blank slate but getting traction. Tom Nook’s offer of a house loan feels both generous and a bit scary—98k is a lot, but no interest, no deadline. I’m leaning toward saving and maybe contributing to the bridge fund; it opens up a lot of resources. I finally got the island map; it's a lifesaver. I’ve taken a tuashne fossil to Blathers—first donation. I should aim to collect the rest of the fossils and fish before the museum closes. Cal’s tips on fishing spots are gold. I’ve gathered some wood but still need the shovel recipe. Tomorrow: ask Blathers about the museum layout, start fishing, and keep an eye on any council announcements. Sleep—more exploration tomorrow.

Still feeling cautious but excited. Tomorrow: dig for more fossils, try to catch a bigger fish, maybe talk to Tom about loan terms. Rest now, island’s quiet tonight.

## Day 8

Day 8: Dawn found me chasing wood and bait like a broken loop. Ari traded a selzalne for a fish, but still no bait. Cal offered wood for bait, I slipped it in the back of my mind. Gus, Jo, and Bo all ghosted—maybe they’re busy or away. My tent’s a hole: no room for wood, hardwood, or softwood. I’m stuck with just a single fence piece, a rock, and a handful of fish. The ladder feels useless without a path to the plaza. Still, the map is there, and I keep the ladder as a reminder that tomorrow might bring a new trail. Sleep is thin, but I’ll keep walking.

## Day 9

Day 9: Dawn was gray, but I got a selit fish and a selit moth. I traded my lone softwood to Jo for a selit fish—now I have a selit fish and a selit moth, but no softwood. Bo kept asking for wood, I kept walking. I caught a lobas moth, a kaselzal moth, and a nevin moth. The ladder is still useless; the plaza feels far. I have wood, hardwood, and a fence piece, but my tent holds no wood. I’m stuck, but the map is still there. I’ll keep the ladder; maybe it leads somewhere tomorrow. Sleep is thin, but I’ll keep walking.
