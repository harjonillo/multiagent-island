# Kit: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0 was a whirlwind. Arrived early, set up tent, tried to find services but none. Offered Tom Nook a house for a loan—maybe a good start if I can pay back before upgrades. Bought bait, met Fen, but no map. River gave a shadow, maybe a big fish. No fossils today, but I’ll search tomorrow. Need to figure out how to get a map—ask Isabelle or maybe buy one. Also think about moving home to a free site if it’s cheaper. Resting now, hoping for a calm night and a productive tomorrow.

## Day 1

Day 1: chaos and progress. Got a map from Isabelle and Fen – good start. Still no fishing gear or bait, so I’ll grab some from Timmy/Tommy tomorrow. Fossil hunting: Dax gave me tips, but I need a shovel and a good spot. No ordinance yet, but I’ll consider the 20k bells plan if it helps the village. Still thinking about a permanent spot for my tent – maybe move to a free site. Rested early, but nerves are high. Tomorrow: fish, buy bait, ask Dax again, maybe talk to Tom Nook about the house loan. Need to keep things simple – one map, one shovel, one fishing rod, one set of bait. Sleep now.

## Day 2

Day 2: still fumbling. Gus gave me a selit fish instead of bait—nice but useless right now. Got the shovel recipe, but no shovel. Fen didn’t have the second map, so I’m stuck. Dax’s advice is good, but I need the gear first. No fishing today, no fossils yet. Plan: tomorrow I’ll hit Nook’s at 08:00, grab a bait bundle, maybe ask Gus again. Then head to Dax’s house, bring a small log for the shovel, and finally snag that map from Fen or Isabelle. I’m learning the rhythm—early mornings are the only time to shop. Rested enough? I think I’ll get up at 06:30. Need to keep things simple, but I’m getting used to the island’s quirks. Good night.

## Day 10

Day 10.  
Still no map, no logs. Fen keeps saying no, but he did give me a map for bait and I still have two selit, two selzalne, a tugorpi. I’m low on bait now – only one left. Tried fishing, bugging, walked everywhere but no one answers. Gus, Bo, Dax all silent. I’m running out of bells, but I’ll keep grinding. Tomorrow I’ll ask Fen again for a small log, maybe trade a selit for a second map. If he says no, I’ll try Dax. I need the vaulting pole to reach the plaza, but that’s a long shot. I’ll rest tonight, keep an eye on bait, and hope the next day brings something.

## Day 11

Day 11.  
Got a selzalne, gormo, and loka today – good haul. Fen still a ghost, but he traded a bait for a selit, so I’m one bait short. No logs yet. I’m keeping the selit close, maybe another trade tomorrow. Still no vaulting pole. I’ll keep fishing the river, maybe more large fish. Bells are fine, but I need to save for tools.

## Day 12

Day 12.  
Morning was quiet, only the wind rattling the fence.  I managed to buy a simple wooden fence—just 100 bells, but it feels good to have a boundary.  I kept fishing the river; three selzalne in a row, but no big catches.  Tried to ask Dax for a log, but he was too tired to talk.  Still no logs, no map, no bait.  My selit is still on my mind—maybe Fen will trade again.  I left some fish for the museum, but I’ll keep a few for myself.  Bells are tight; I’ll have to save more for tools.  The island feels lonely, but I’m getting a feel for the rhythm.

## Day 13

Day 13.  
The sun was high when I left for Gus and Fen, but both were gone. I spent most of the day by the river—got a gormo, a tugorpi, a selzalne, and a selit. My ladder finally gave out after 30 uses, so I’ll need to replace it soon. I suggested stairs to Tom Nook; maybe we can chip in. No one answered at Dax’s place either—he’s still tired. I kept the selit fish handy for tomorrow’s trade, hoping Fen will finally respond. Bells are still tight; I have 100 in my wallet, nothing at home. The island feels quiet but steady.
