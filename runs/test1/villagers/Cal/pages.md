# Cal: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0 was a whirlwind. I landed at dawn, set up my tent on the lower ground east (site‑8), and realized the island’s services are still figuring out their schedule. No Resident Services, no Nook’s Cranny open yet – I’ll have to wait until 8 AM. I managed to catch a selit and a loka fish, and spent 300 bells on bait. Met Dax and Fen at the river; both say no ordinance yet, but they’ll keep an eye out. I need to keep fishing for larger ones tomorrow and definitely ask Isabelle about the ordinance in the morning. Also, I’ll check the river after using the new bait. If I can get more fish, maybe I’ll trade for something useful. Time to rest.

## Day 1

Day 1, 20 h.  
Morning: Isabelle’s big map and the “no ordinance” deal. 20000 bells for a rule?  I’ll see what everyone thinks tomorrow.  Dax asked about Blathers, I pointed him to the center.  Fen wanted a map; I handed over the one from Isabelle.  Fishing: three selzalne, a loka, then a missed small fish when Gus was nearby.  Caught another loka at 15:00.  No fish after that – river looks empty.  Tried to visit Blathers; nothing but his museum.  Rested at 17:00.  

Thoughts: The ordinance is still a gamble.  I need more fish before I can trade or ask for better bait.  Maybe talk to Dax and Fen about fishing spots.  Keep the map handy – it’s the only way to know where the shops are.  Tomorrow: ask Isabelle again if anything changed, try for a very large fish before dusk.  Bells: 700 left, 300 spent on bait.  Feeling hopeful but cautious.

## Day 2

Day 2, 21 h.  
River: one selit with bait, then nothing.  No bait left – need to ask Dax or Jo.  Blathers still empty, no donations yet.  Felt stuck.  The map is useless for fishing; maybe different spot.  I’m running low on bells – 700 left, 300 spent.  I’ll try to trade for bait tomorrow, ask Dax again, maybe steal a small amount from Jo.  Still curious about Isabelle’s ordinance – will see if anyone talks about it.  Need more fish to feel useful.  Hope tomorrow brings a big catch.

## Day 3

Day 3, 03 h.  
Sun’s up, but still no bait. Dax keeps his wood, Jo’s home empty again. I’m stuck with selzalne and loka, no selit. I’ll trade the two selzalne for a wood and see if Dax will give me a bait later. The river feels empty—maybe the map’s wrong. I’ll keep the ladder and net ready, just in case. Isabelle’s ordinance still a mystery; maybe it’s about fishing limits. I’m low on bells, but I’ve got a few extra wood and fish to trade. I’ll try to catch a big one before sunset, but I’ll also ask Dax for a spot. Tonight I’ll rest, maybe the next morning I’ll get a better idea of where the fish are.

## Day 6

Day 6, 23 h.  
Morning: Gus gave me wood—small win. Dax still silent, maybe tired or busy. I’ve got no bait, no fish shadows, no chance to catch anything. My fishing rod and net sit idle; the ladder and shovel feel useless tonight. I’m stuck with selit, loka, selzalne, and a lone piren. Bells are decent, but I can’t afford a bait yet. I’ll keep an eye on Dax tomorrow, maybe trade those selzalne for more wood or bait. I’ll also try Jo again—she’s been evasive but maybe she’ll have something. The river looks empty, but I’ll keep walking, maybe a new spot appears. Sleep now; hope tomorrow brings a splash.

## Day 7

Day 7, 23 h.

Plan: trade 2 selzalne for 1 wood tomorrow, then buy more bait. Need to finish the bridge to open new spots; maybe chip in 10k. Also consider moving Tom’s house if I can afford the loan—could get extra storage. Sleep now, hope tomorrow brings more fish shadows and a reply from Jo.

## Day 11

Day 11, 23 h.  
Morning: nothing from Dax—still no reply. Went fishing twice, got a moitbas and a gorpika, and a miruru from the pond. Bug‑catching netted an ashrumo moth near rock 6; I’ll trade that for softwood tomorrow. Went to Jo, got a nod but no trade—just offered a bit of fish, but she’s tired. Evening: caught a tubas fish in the sea. Still only 100 bells, no new items. I’m running low on rope and wood, and the bridge’s still a block. I’ll ask Dax tomorrow for any spare wood and maybe trade a selzalne for softwood. Need to keep the bridge funded.
