# Ike: what they wrote to themselves, night by night

## Day 0

Day 0 was a whirlwind. Arrived early, set up the tent, and hit the river—got a selzalne, but no big stuff today. Bought bait, a fence, and talked to Ari. Tom Nook offered a huge loan for a house and a cheap move. I’m leaning toward the house; the loan is tempting but I’ll need to pay it back quickly. No fossils yet, but I’ll scour the cliffs tomorrow. Weather’s a factor—rain could kill the fish, so I’ll keep an eye on the sky. Ladder in mind for higher spots. If I can get the bridge funded, that’ll open up more of the island. For now, just rest and plan the next day's hunt.

## Day 1

Day 1, sun’s gone down.

Sleep tight, keep the ladder handy.

## Day 7

Lost one selzalne in trade, but that’s the price of progress.  
Still no fishing bait, so I’m stuck in a loop: I need bait to fish, I need fish to trade for bait.  
Will post another bait request on the board tonight.  
If I can’t get bait, I’ll try to fish for gormo next morning—maybe that will fetch some coins.  
Vaulting pole still missing; I’ll salvage a stick and craft a crude one tomorrow.  
Plan: trade wood for a ladder with Bo if possible, then head to the plaza.  
Sleep early, wake at 20:00, hope for a trade.  
— Ike

## Day 8

Day 8, night.  
No luck getting the bait—Ari keeps offering fish, I keep offering wood. The trade loop is still stuck. I finally got a wood back from Ari, so I have 3 now, but the bait is still missing. Cal is sleeping, no answer. I’m exhausted; will try to fish for gormo tomorrow, hoping a single fish will fetch some bells. I’ll keep posting the bait request on the board tonight. I need to salvage a stick for a pole, but first I’ll see if anyone has a spare ladder to trade for a ladder. Maybe Bo will help.  
— Ike

## Day 9

Day 9, night.  
I’m still stuck on the bait.  Ari keeps swapping wood for fish, and Cal is still out of the woods.  The pond gave me a miruru and an ashrumo moth, but I’m still 0 bells.  I’ll try the fishing rod again tomorrow; maybe a gormo will fetch something.  No trades today, so I keep my 3 woods and 1 fish.  I’ll post a request on the board tonight – “Need 1 fishing bait, will trade 1 wood.”  I’m tired but hopeful.  Maybe Bo will have a ladder, or someone will bring a spare.  The island feels quiet, but the water’s still alive.  I’ll keep an eye on the rocks for more bugs and the pond for more fish.  

I’ll sleep early, maybe get some rest.

## Day 10

Day 10, night.  
The day was a loop of nothing but fish and wood.  I kept trading with Ari, but he never had bait.  I finally caught a loka, a tugorpi, and a lovin’ fish without bait—maybe the river is generous today.  My tent is still full of wood, and my wallet is still empty.  I still don’t know how to get to the plaza, so I’ll keep trying the pole.  I’m going to head to Nook’s Cranny tomorrow to buy a bait for Ari, hoping he’ll have something in return.  If I can’t pay, I’ll offer wood again.  The island feels quiet, but the water’s alive.  Sleep now; tomorrow is another chance.

## Day 11

Day 11, night.  
Sun rose over the river, and I chased the ashrumo moth like a kid in a forest. It was worth the trip—two moths, a tugorpi, and a lovin’ fish, all without bait. I traded one wood for a fishing bait from Ari, and he seemed happier when I left. The rock at 07:00 was a goldmine—6,750 bells tucked into my tent. I hammered a hardwood tree and found clay, stone, and an iron nugget near rock 6. The river is generous today, but I still have no bells in my hand—just the 6,750 in the tent. Tomorrow I’ll head to the plaza, try to buy more bait, and maybe ask Ari for another trade. The island feels quiet, but the water’s alive, and I’m tired but hopeful.
