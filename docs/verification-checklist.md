# Verification checklist

- Who is the original source? "Kakilala sa city hall" is not a source.
- Can you find the same claim on an official channel?
- Is the claim broader than official announcements usually are? Blanket
  "all levels, whole city" claims are a warning sign.
- If you post before verifying, label it unverified and say what you are checking.
- When you learn the correct version, post a clear correction.
